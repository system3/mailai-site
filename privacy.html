mailai Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

mailai ("the app") is a desktop email client that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Google account data: with your permission, the app uses Google OAuth to access your Gmail messages (scope https://mail.google.com/) and your contacts (contacts.readonly, contacts.other.readonly) so that it can show, search, organize and send your mail and suggest recipients.
  • Other mail accounts: if you add Microsoft or IMAP/SMTP accounts, the app accesses those mailboxes in the same way.

2. How data is stored

Messages, contacts and OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer. The developer does not operate any server that receives, stores or analyzes your mail or account data.

3. AI features

AI features (such as summaries, briefings and the AI assistant) are optional and run only when you turn them on. When you use them, the content needed for the request (for example the text of the selected messages) is sent directly from your computer to the AI provider you configured (for example OpenAI/ChatGPT or another provider you choose), or processed by a local AI model if you choose one. That provider's own privacy policy applies to data sent to it. Nothing is sent to an AI provider unless you enable and use these features.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell your data, does not share it with third parties except the AI provider you choose as described above, and does not use it for advertising.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

mailai's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Removing your data

You can remove an account in the app, which deletes its locally stored data, and you can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all remaining local data.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: intblue3@gmail.com
